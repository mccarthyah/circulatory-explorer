Changes and scope
-----------------
This project selects and classifies structures; converts coordinates and units;
recomputes normals; exports meshes as glTF; simplifies meshes for lower detail;
clips and filters regional views; and derives a tour path and catalogue from
the source data. Regional cut surfaces are open. The original models and our
adaptations are not a complete clinical anatomy reference.

The license notice above applies to the anatomy data and its adaptations, not
a blanket statement that all application code uses the same license. When
redistributing, retain the credits, license notices, source information, and
indication of changes, and observe the applicable license terms.

Reference: Mitsuhashi N, Fujieda K, Tamura T, Kawamoto S, Takagi T, Okubo K.
BodyParts3D: 3D structure database for anatomical concepts. Nucleic Acids Res.
2008 Oct 3. PMID: 18835852. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18835852/

Optional Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D organ comparison
-------------------------------------------------
The optional 17-organ layer is adapted from the organs.glb and anatomy.json
of Vessels, by mauupa0 and contributors, commit
6c9aced83d264a3ed0155c2c2a1c78f2de6c5e16:
https://github.com/mauupa0/vessels-3d/tree/6c9aced83d264a3ed0155c2c2a1c78f2de6c5e16/public/anatomy
Source SHA-256: organs.glb 14dcd2ee6c1a42f90033caf9497cfce01f266d2bd0cff6626b4937d3c2c95bef;
anatomy.json 47dc43d7bfbac425b2ebb8c632e411a058c42db1e8f081a40b901b455bb5c94c.

Z-Anatomy, The libre 3D atlas of anatomy: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The derivative identifies kidneys, brain and small intestine as BodyParts3D,
and the other organs as Z-Anatomy; it states that Z-Anatomy's NC-restricted
items were excluded. Z-Anatomy itself derives from BodyParts3D. The derivative
credits BodyParts3D to DBCLS, Okubo K. et al., notes the current CC BY 4.0 and
older CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan notices, and distributes its derived files under
CC BY-SA 4.0. The source and legacy licence discrepancy above still applies;
no determination of historical licence compatibility is claimed here.

Changes: the derivative registered, smoothed and simplified its sources,
merged them by layer and compressed them. We decoded and split the merged organ
mesh into 17 selectable meshes, rotated and translated the coordinates,
recomputed normals and exported them as glTF. The translation is based on
rough organ-centre matches; this is an approximate visual comparison of
different bodies, not a validated anatomical registration or clinical model.
