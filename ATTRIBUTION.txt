Legacy source notice and adapted assets
---------------------------------------
The headers of the supplemental IS-A source OBJ files obtained from the mirror
state:

  BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science licensed under
  CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan.

CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/
The mirror also describes its BodyParts3D data under that legacy license.

The official page now states CC BY 4.0; the old OBJ notice and the mirror state
CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. The official 20130619 release README, updated in 2025,
also names CC BY 4.0 but links to the old CC BY-SA deed in its license section.
This notice records the discrepancy; it does not determine whether the current
statement applies to every historical or mirrored file. For these adapted
anatomy assets, this project retains the legacy notice and distributes its
contributions under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan as a conservative approach. Contact DBCLS
or obtain qualified legal advice for a definitive assessment of the exact files
and intended distribution.

Changes and scope
-----------------
This project selects and classifies structures; converts coordinates and units;
recomputes normals; exports meshes as glTF; simplifies meshes for lower detail;
clips and filters regional views; and derives a tour path and catalogue from
the source data. Regional cut surfaces are open. The original models and our
adaptations are not a complete clinical anatomy reference.

The license notice above applies to the anatomy data and its adaptations, not
a blanket statement that all application code uses the same license. When
redistributing, retain the credits, license notices, source information, and
indication of changes, and observe the applicable license terms.
